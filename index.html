About Us

We are a dynamic team of computer science enthusiasts dedicated to solving real-world problems through innovative technology solutions.

Who We Are

Team Rohini Nakshatra was formed in 2023 by four passionate BTech Computer Science students who share a common vision of leveraging technology to create meaningful impact.

Our team name draws inspiration from the Rohini nakshatra, symbolizing growth, creativity, and the ability to navigate challenges - qualities that define our approach to problem-solving.

We combine our diverse skills in software development, AI/ML, web technologies, and design to build solutions that matter.
