Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What types of projects do you take on?

+

We specialize in web and mobile applications, AI/ML solutions, blockchain projects, and IoT systems. We're particularly interested in projects with social impact, educational technology, and innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Do you offer internships?

+

Yes, we occasionally offer internship opportunities to students who are passionate about technology and innovation. Interns work on real projects alongside team members and gain hands-on experience in software development, project management, and teamwork.

Can we collaborate on hackathons?

+

Absolutely! We're always open to collaborating with other teams or individuals for hackathons. We believe diverse perspectives lead to better solutions. Reach out to us at least a week before the hackathon to discuss potential collaboration.

What's your typical project timeline?

+

Timelines vary based on project complexity. Simple MVPs can be developed in 2-4 weeks, while more complex solutions may take 2-3 months. We follow agile methodology with regular updates and milestones to ensure timely delivery.
